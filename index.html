FAQ

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ varies by product and customization. Tell us the items and quantities you need and we will confirm.

Can you provide samples?

Yes. Sample terms (cost, freight) depend on the product and are confirmed in the quotation.

Do you offer OEM / private label?

Yes — logo marking, custom packaging and custom designs are available, subject to MOQ.

Which trade terms and payment methods do you accept?

FOB, CIF and DDP are supported. Payment terms are agreed per order.
